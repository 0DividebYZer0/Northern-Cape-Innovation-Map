# Contributing to the South African Innovation Landscape

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the South African Innovation Landscape project! This is an open-source initiative designed to map and support the innovation ecosystem in South Africa. We believe that a sustainable project requires the collaboration of **Government, Civil Society, Academia, and the Private Sector** (the Quadruple Helix model).

## How You Can Contribute

We welcome contributions from everyone, regardless of their background or expertise. Here are some ways each sector can contribute:

### 🏛️ Government & Public Sector
- **Data Sharing:** Provide open data on public innovation hubs, funding opportunities, and policy frameworks.
- **Policy Alignment:** Help align the platform's goals with national and provincial innovation strategies.
- **Sustainability:** Explore ways to integrate the platform into public service delivery and local economic development.

### 🤝 Civil Society & Community
- **Community Mapping:** Help us identify local innovators, grassroots projects, and community-led initiatives.
- **User Feedback:** Tell us how the platform can better serve the needs of community members and small business owners.
- **Advocacy:** Promote the platform to ensure it reaches those who can benefit most from it.

### 🎓 Academia & Research
- **Data Analysis:** Use the platform's data for research on the South African innovation ecosystem.
- **Methodology:** Help us refine our mapping and verification processes (e.g., the "Pulse" verification system).
- **Student Projects:** Encourage students to contribute to the codebase or use the platform for their studies.

### 🏢 Private Sector & Industry
- **Sponsorship:** Support the platform's operational costs through our VIP Sponsor program.
- **Technical Expertise:** Contribute code, design, or infrastructure to improve the platform's performance and features.
- **Mentorship:** Offer mentorship and resources to the innovators mapped on the platform.

## Getting Started

1.  **Explore the Platform:** Visit the [live app](https://ais-pre-lnqp3k7hz6wlef6xdpfb6w-158149471293.europe-west3.run.app) to see how it works.

## Sustainability

To ensure the long-term sustainability of the project, we are exploring various models, including:
- **Public-Private Partnerships (PPPs)**
- **Community-Led Governance**
- **Open Data Commons**

We welcome your ideas on how to make this project more sustainable and impactful for all South Africans.
